# Product Specification Verification Record

Internal record for Freshland Exports. Not linked from the website.

Last updated: 2026-10-12.

## Rule applied

A technical value appears on a product page only if it is Freshland Exports' own confirmed specification. Another supplier's figures, industry ranges and regulatory limits are not treated as the company's specification. Where no confirmed value existed, the row was removed rather than shown as "To be confirmed".

Status labels used below:

- **Verified product-specific specification:** none were found. No public source states Freshland Exports' own grade, mesh size, moisture, shelf life, curcumin, variety or origin.
- **Regulatory limit, not company spec:** an official maximum or minimum (FSSAI, Codex, BIS). Recorded here for comparison; not displayed.
- **Removed:** the placeholder row was deleted from the page.

### Not found

- **Psyllium seed:** no FSSAI spice standard.
- **Moringa leaf powder:** no standard found.
- **Grade, mesh size and shelf life:** these are commercial or product-specific values, not set by the standards above.
- **Fruit and vegetable varieties, and origin:** company sourcing facts. Only the business can confirm them.
- **Chilli colour (ASTA):** no standard for dried chilli or chilli powder found.
- **Not checked:** Codex standards for cloves, ginger, chilli, cardamom, nutmeg and turmeric (fao.org blocked access); ISO standards; Spices Board and AGMARK grade rules.
